Cutdeck

A simple video editor. Trim, add captions, speed up, export. Your videos never leave your computer.

Download for Windows Mac M1 and newer Mac Intel Linux

Also: .deb for Ubuntu, .msi for Windows, older versions.

What it does

  • Auto captionsEnglish, Hindi or Hinglish. Fix any word, pick a style, burn it in or save an .srt.
  • Steady shaky clipsSmooth out handheld footage with one setting.
  • Speed it up or slow it downAnywhere from 0.25× to 100×.
  • Remove the backgroundCut out people or objects, no green screen needed.
  • Make it look good12 filters, text titles, and background music with fades.
  • Any shape, up to 4KReels, square or widescreen. Exports a normal MP4.

Opening it the first time

Cutdeck isn’t signed by Apple or Microsoft yet, so your computer will ask once. It’s safe.

Mac says it can’t be opened

Drag Cutdeck to Applications. Then right-click it, choose Open, and Open again.

Still blocked? Paste this in Terminal, then open it normally: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Cutdeck.app

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”

Click More info, then Run anyway.

Linux

Make the file runnable with chmod +x Cutdeck-linux-x86_64.AppImage, then double-click it. On Ubuntu you can also use the .deb.
