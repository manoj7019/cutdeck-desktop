Featured

The parts people open first

Three tools that are actually in the editor, not a roadmap.

Speech

hello दोस्तों

Auto captions

Whisper runs offline on the clip’s audio. Auto-detect, English, Hindi, or Hinglish in Roman script. Words land on the timeline so you can edit, split and retime them. Styles are Clean, Bold yellow, Boxed and Word pop, in Noto Sans or Poppins, top, middle or bottom. Export burns them into the MP4, or save an .srt. The speech model downloads once, then stays on disk.

Camera

Stabilize shaky footage

A two-pass smooth (vidstab): Cutdeck reads the camera motion, then renders a steadier copy. Low keeps intentional pans. Medium is the default. High looks more like a locked tripod and zooms in a little so the edges stay filled. Trims, speed and captions stay on the same clip, and you can switch back to the original.

Time

100×

Speed, slow or ridiculous

Any clip from 0.25× to 100×, with a slider and exact value. Export retimes in one FFmpeg pass and only keeps the frames that land in the output. From 50× up, Turbo reads keyframes so a long clip finishes much faster. At 8× and above the clip’s own sound mutes (you can turn it back on); put music under it instead.

The rest of the editor

Everything else it does

The same tools as the app’s rail: canvas, trim, filters, cutout, text, music, export and projects.

Timeline

  • Import video and photos, drag to reorder, trim with handles, split, duplicate, delete.
  • Undo and redo. Drag clips, titles and captions on the timeline.
  • Photos sit for a duration you set. Video in, out and speed define the length.
  • Transition into a clip: a hard cut, or a short fade from black.

Canvas

  • Frame: original, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5, 16:9, 4:3 or 3:4.
  • The space around a clip can be a blurred copy of the picture or a flat colour.
  • Fit the whole clip inside the frame, or fill and crop it.

Look

  • Twelve filters: Original, Vivid, Golden, Frost, Matte, Vintage, Mono, Noir, Dusk, Mint, Cinema, Pop.
  • Sliders for brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth and vignette.

Transform

  • Rotate, mirror, zoom, and drag the picture around on the preview.
  • Reset puts the clip back to centre, zoom 1, no rotation.

Background removal

  • On-device cutout with U²-Net (a fast model and a slower, cleaner one) for people, pets, products and other salient objects.
  • Replace the background with blur, a colour, another image, or the canvas.
  • Green-screen and blue-screen key, with a tolerance slider and an eyedropper.
  • Photos can be saved as a transparent PNG. Video cutout is composited at export. Nothing is uploaded.

Text

  • Titles in Anton, Manrope, Bebas Neue, Playfair Display, Pacifico or Caveat.
  • Styles: Plain, Shadow, Outline, Label. Colour, size, and show/hide times.
  • Drag a title on the preview. This is separate from auto captions.

Sound

  • Per-clip volume, including mute, and mute-all.
  • One music bed under the whole edit: volume, song offset, where it starts, fade in and out.
  • Music can be mp3, m4a, wav, flac, ogg, or the audio from a video file.

Export

  • MP4, H.264 video and AAC audio.
  • 720p, 1080p, 2K or 4K. 24, 30 or 60 fps. Smaller file or high quality.
  • Uses the fastest encoder that works on the machine: NVIDIA NVENC, Apple VideoToolbox, Intel Quick Sync, AMD AMF, or x264 on the CPU.
  • Optional burn-in of captions. You can also write a separate .srt.

Projects

  • Save and open .cutdeck projects. Media stays where it already is on disk.
  • Autosaved drafts, with rename and delete, so a crash doesn’t take the edit.
  • If the preview can’t play a format such as iPhone HEVC, Cutdeck makes a 720p stand-in. Export still uses the original file.

Install

First launch

The downloads above are the latest release. The builds are not code-signed or notarized yet, so each system asks once.

macOS: “cannot be opened” or “unidentified developer”

Open the disk image, drag Cutdeck to Applications, then right-click the app and choose Open, then Open again. If Gatekeeper still blocks it, run this in Terminal and open the app normally:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Cutdeck.app

Apple Silicon uses the Apple Silicon disk image. Intel Macs use the Intel one. Signing and notarization need an Apple Developer account; until those secrets are set, this workaround is expected. See the repository README.

Windows SmartScreen

SmartScreen says Windows protected your PC because the installer isn’t signed. Choose More info, then Run anyway. The setup exe is the usual install. An .msi is on the same release.

Linux

The AppImage is one file: chmod +x Cutdeck-linux-x86_64.AppImage and run it. The .deb is for Debian and Ubuntu (sudo apt install ./Cutdeck-linux-amd64.deb) and pulls in WebKitGTK. FFmpeg, the cutout runtime and the caption engine are inside the app.

Cutdeck is free. Source and releases.
